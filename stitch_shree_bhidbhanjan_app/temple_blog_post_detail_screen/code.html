The Spiritual Significance of Aarti

Pujya Swami Ji

Oct 24, 2023 • 5 min read

Aarti is not merely a ritual; it is a profound expression of devotion, a moment when the individual soul connects deeply with the supreme consciousness. Derived from the Sanskrit words 'Aa' (towards or completely) and 'Rati' (right or virtue), Aarti signifies complete love and surrender.

"When you offer the light to the Divine, you are essentially offering your own inner light, your soul, removing the darkness of ignorance."

The flame, usually lit using camphor or ghee, is symbolic. Camphor burns completely without leaving any residue, representing the ideal spiritual state where the ego is completely dissolved, leaving no trace behind.

As the Aarti is performed, the rhythmic chanting, the ringing of bells, and the fragrance of incense create an atmosphere that engages all five senses, focusing them entirely on the deity.
