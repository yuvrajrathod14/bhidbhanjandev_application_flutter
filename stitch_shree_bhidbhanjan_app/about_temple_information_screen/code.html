History & Heritage

Established in 1924, Shri Hanuman Mandir has stood as a beacon of faith, devotion, and community service for nearly a century. The temple was built by local devotees who experienced divine miracles during its inception.

Architecture

The temple showcases traditional Nagara style architecture with intricate sandstone carvings depicting tales from the Ramayana.

Management & Trust

The temple is managed by the Shri Hanuman Mandir Charitable Trust. The committee oversees daily rituals, festival organizations, and various philanthropic activities in the region.
