About This Event

Join us for the auspicious occasion of Maha Shivratri, a night dedicated to Lord Shiva. Experience profound spiritual energy through continuous chanting, special Abhishekams, and divine bhajans. The night-long vigil (Jagaran) will culminate in the grand Maha Aarti at dawn. Devotees are encouraged to fast and participate in the continuous "Om Namah Shivaya" japa.
